Is Water Still Coming In? Do This While You Call

  • Shut off the main water valve, usually in the basement or utility room near the water heater.
  • Turn off power to wet rooms at the breaker if you can reach it from a dry spot.
  • Stay out of sewage or flood water.
  • Take photos of the water and the source.

Contact FAQs

Do you answer the phone at night?

Yes. Call (385) 488-1110 any hour, including weekends and holidays.

Can I send photos instead of calling?

Yes. Use the form above for non-urgent damage. For water that is still coming in, call right away.

Do you serve my city?

We serve Midvale and nearby Salt Lake County cities including Murray, Sandy, Cottonwood Heights, West Jordan, Taylorsville and South Jordan. Call if you are close by and not on the list.

What should I have ready when I call?

Your address, what happened, whether water is still coming in, and your insurance company name if you plan to file a claim.

Can I call just for advice?

Yes. If you are not sure whether a leak needs professional drying, call and describe what you see. We will tell you honestly whether you need us.

Do you help businesses and property managers too?

Yes. We handle offices, retail, restaurants and apartment buildings across Midvale and nearby cities.

I rent my home. Can I still call you?

Yes. Call us for the emergency, and tell your landlord or property manager right away. We can work with them and their insurer on the drying and repairs.

Can you start before my insurance company calls back?

Yes. Your policy expects you to prevent more damage, so water removal and drying can start right away. We document everything so your adjuster sees exactly what was done.
