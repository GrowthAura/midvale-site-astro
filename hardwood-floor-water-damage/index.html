Hardwood Floor Water Damage Drying in Midvale

Solid hardwood can often be dried in place and saved if drying starts within a day or two. Our Midvale crew uses floor drying mats that pull water up from under the boards, then dries slowly and evenly so the wood settles back instead of cracking. Call (385) 488-1110.

Quick answer: Cupping, where the edges of the boards rise, can often be reversed with proper drying. Buckling, where boards lift off the subfloor, usually means replacement. Engineered wood and laminate that have swollen usually cannot be saved.

Can Your Wet Hardwood Be Saved?

Your floor has the best chance when:

  • It is solid hardwood, not engineered wood or laminate
  • The water was clean, like a burst pipe or supply line
  • Drying starts within 24 to 48 hours
  • The subfloor is not soaked through

Sewage or outside flood water on wood floors usually means removal.

How We Dry Hardwood Floors in Midvale Homes

  • Measure moisture in the boards and the subfloor, and take a dry reading from an unaffected room.
  • Extract standing water and water between the boards.
  • Lay floor drying mats that seal to the wood and use suction to pull water up from below.
  • Control the room with dehumidifiers, so the wood dries evenly top and bottom.
  • Check readings daily until the wood matches the dry reading.
  • Wait, then refinish. Boards are sanded and refinished only after they are fully dry and settled.

Why Slow, Even Drying Matters in Utah

Utah's air is dry, which helps. But drying wood too fast with high heat can split boards and make cupping worse. Sanding before the wood is dry often leaves the floor crowned. We dry at a controlled pace and do not over-dry the wood.

Laminate, Engineered Wood and Vinyl Plank

  • Laminate: the fiberboard core swells and does not go back. Swollen laminate is replaced.
  • Engineered wood: a thin wood layer over plywood or fiberboard. Light wetting can sometimes be dried; swollen or peeling boards are replaced.
  • Luxury vinyl plank: the planks themselves resist water, but water gets trapped underneath. Planks can often be lifted, the floor dried, and the planks reused.

What Affects How Long Hardwood Drying Takes

  • Wood type and thickness: solid oak dries slower than thin engineered boards
  • Subfloor: plywood over a crawl space or basement dries differently than wood over concrete
  • How long the water sat before drying started
  • Finish: some finishes slow how fast moisture leaves the top of the board
  • Season: cold winter rooms need extra heat for even drying

Hardwood Floor Water Damage FAQs

Will cupped hardwood go back flat?

Often, yes, once the moisture under the boards is removed and the wood dries evenly. Some floors still need light sanding and refinishing.

How long does it take to dry hardwood floors?

Often about 1 to 2 weeks, depending on how wet the boards and subfloor got.

Is laminate ruined if it gets wet?

If the edges have swollen, yes. Laminate does not shrink back after its core swells.

Can I sand my floor flat right away?

No. Sanding wet wood often leaves it crowned once it finishes drying. Wait until moisture readings are back to normal.

Does insurance cover water-damaged hardwood?

Most policies cover sudden water damage, like a burst pipe or appliance leak. Slow, long-term leaks often are not covered.

Should I remove the baseboards on a wet wood floor?

Often, yes. Removing baseboards lets air reach the edges of the floor and the bottom of the wall. We label and save them for reinstalling when possible.

Can water under hardwood cause mold?

Yes. Water trapped between the boards and the subfloor can grow mold if it is not dried fast. Drying mats pull that water out from below.

Why did my floor cup more after drying started?

That is common. Cupping can show up or get worse during the first days of drying as moisture moves through the boards. It is a reason to keep drying evenly, not to give up on the floor or sand it early.

Can you dry hardwood from below?

Yes, when there is a basement or crawl space under the floor. Drying the subfloor from underneath while mats pull from above speeds things up and helps the boards settle flat.

Can engineered hardwood be saved?

Sometimes. If only the surface got wet and the core is not swollen, it can be dried. If the plywood or fiberboard core is saturated or the top layer is peeling, those boards are replaced.
