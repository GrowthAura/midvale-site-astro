Why Salt Lake Valley Homes Share the Same Risks

The whole valley deals with the same seasons: winter freeze-thaw that bursts pipes, ice dams on snowy roofs, spring snowmelt that raises groundwater near the Jordan River and the canyon creeks, and summer monsoon storms that overload storm drains. What changes from city to city is the age of the homes, how close they sit to a river or creek, and whether they have basements and window wells.

Why does it matter that your crew is based in Midvale?
