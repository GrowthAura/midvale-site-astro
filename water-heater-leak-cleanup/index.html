A leaking or burst water heater can dump 40 to 50 gallons in minutes, and keep refilling until the water is shut off. Our Midvale crew extracts the water, dries the utility room, walls and floors around it, and gets the space ready for your new heater. Call (385) 488-1110.

Quick answer: Where the leak is tells you how urgent it is. Water at the top usually comes from a loose connection or the T&P relief valve and can often be repaired. Water pooling under the tank usually means the tank itself has corroded through, and it needs to be replaced.

Water Heater Leaking? Shut It Down Safely

  • Turn off the cold water supply at the valve on the pipe above the heater. If you can't reach it, close the main shut-off.
  • Turn off the energy: set a gas heater to "off" or "pilot", or switch off the breaker for an electric heater.
  • Let it cool. The water inside can be hot enough to scald.
  • Keep away from wet outlets and the furnace if water is spreading.
  • Take photos of the heater, the label and the wet area, then call (385) 488-1110.

Why Water Heaters Fail in Salt Lake Valley Homes

The Salt Lake Valley has hard water. Minerals settle as sediment at the bottom of the tank, the anode rod wears out, and the steel tank starts to corrode from the inside. Many Midvale homes also have the water heater in a finished basement or utility closet, so a leak spreads straight into carpet and drywall.

Warning signs before a tank fails: rust or water on top of the tank, a drip from the relief valve pipe or the drain valve, rusty or cloudy hot water, rumbling or popping sounds, and damp spots near the floor drain. A tank rarely fails all at once; it usually shows these signs first.

Stop the Next Water Heater Leak

  • Flush the tank once a year to clear sediment
  • Test the T&P relief valve once a year
  • Have the anode rod checked every few years
  • Set the heater in a drain pan piped to a floor drain
  • Add a water leak sensor on the floor next to the heater

Water Heater Leak FAQs

Is a leaking water heater an emergency?

A leak under the tank is. It usually means the tank is failing and could release all of its water. A small drip at a connection is less urgent but should be fixed soon.

How do I shut off my water heater?

Close the cold water valve above the heater, then turn the gas control to off or pilot, or switch off the breaker for an electric heater.

Does insurance cover water heater water damage?

Most homeowners policies cover sudden water damage from a burst tank. They usually do not pay for the heater itself if it simply wore out.

Why is water coming from the pipe on the side of my water heater?

That pipe is the T&P relief valve discharge. It opens when temperature or pressure gets too high, or drips when the valve is worn. Have a plumber check it.

How long do water heaters last in Utah?

Many tank heaters last about 8 to 12 years. Hard water can shorten that if the tank is not flushed.

Can I still use hot water while my water heater is leaking?

Not if the tank itself is leaking. Shut off the cold water supply and the energy until a plumber checks it. A small drip at a connection may be safe to use briefly, but have it fixed soon.

Should I repair or replace a leaking water heater?

Leaks at connections, the drain valve or the T&P valve can often be repaired. A leak from the tank body means the tank has failed and needs replacement.

How can I tell how long my water heater was leaking?

Rust stains on the carpet from the tack strip, swollen baseboards, a musty smell or a dark water line on drywall mean the leak has been going for days. Our moisture map shows how far it spread.

Does a tankless water heater leak too?

Yes, though less often. Leaks usually come from fittings, the pressure relief valve or a cracked heat exchanger after a freeze. The cleanup is the same: stop the water, extract and dry.

Is water from a water heater clean?

It starts as clean water. Sediment from the bottom of the tank can make it dirty, and after a day or two in carpet it is treated as Category 2 and cleaned before drying.
