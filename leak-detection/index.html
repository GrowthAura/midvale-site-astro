Hidden Water Leak Detection in Midvale

A hidden leak behind a wall, under a floor or in a ceiling can run for weeks before you see it. Our Midvale crew finds where the water is with thermal imaging and moisture meters, maps how far it has spread, and gives you a clear report before anything is opened up. Call (385) 488-1110.

Quick answer: If your water bill jumps, you smell something musty, or paint bubbles with no obvious cause, you may have a hidden leak. A thermal camera shows cool, wet areas inside walls and ceilings, and moisture meters confirm them, so only the right spot gets opened.

Signs of a Hidden Water Leak

  • Water bill higher than usual with no change in use
  • Musty smell in one room or closet
  • Bubbling paint, peeling wallpaper or soft drywall
  • Warped baseboards or flooring that lifts at the seams
  • Stains on ceilings below bathrooms or laundry rooms
  • The sound of running water when every tap is off
  • A warm spot on a slab floor, which can mean a hot water line leak

More signs, room by room: hidden water damage signs.

Try the Water Meter Test First

  • Turn off every faucet, appliance and the irrigation system.
  • Find your water meter, usually in a box near the street.
  • Write down the reading, or watch the small leak indicator dial.
  • Wait 1 to 2 hours without using any water.
  • If the reading changed or the dial moved, water is leaking somewhere.

Tools We Use to Find Leaks Without Tearing Out Walls

ToolWhat it shows
Thermal imaging cameraTemperature differences that point to wet areas inside walls, ceilings and floors
Pinless moisture meterScans surfaces for moisture without making holes
Pin moisture meterConfirms exact moisture content in drywall and wood
Thermo-hygrometerHumidity and temperature in a room or wall cavity
Borescope cameraA small camera through a tiny hole to look inside a wall

A thermal camera does not see water directly. It shows temperature patterns, which we confirm with meters before we call anything a leak.

Leaks under concrete: a hot water line leaking under a slab often shows up as a warm spot on the floor in a thermal image. Plumbers then use acoustic listening tools to pinpoint the break before any concrete is cut. We map the wet area so the plumber knows where to listen.

Your Moisture Map and Report

After the inspection, you get a moisture map with photos showing:

  • Where the water is and how far it has spread
  • Which materials are wet and which are dry
  • The likely source, so your plumber knows where to look
  • What needs drying, and what may need removal

This report is also useful for your insurance claim.

What Happens After We Find the Leak

  • Your plumber repairs the pipe, fitting or fixture.
  • We dry the wet area with targeted drying, often through small holes.
  • If mold has started, we contain and remove it.
  • We repair the drywall, flooring or ceiling.

What Affects How Long Leak Detection Takes

  • How many areas show signs of water
  • Access: finished basements, tile and built-ins can hide the source
  • Whether the leak is active: a running leak is easier to trace than an old one
  • Size of the home or building
  • Slab or crawl space: leaks under concrete need more testing

Leak Detection FAQs

How do you find a leak behind a wall?

We scan with a thermal camera for cool, wet patterns, then confirm with moisture meters. If needed, a small borescope camera looks inside through a tiny hole.

Can a water meter show a hidden leak?

Yes. If the meter moves while all water is off, water is leaking somewhere on your property.

What does a moisture map show?

It shows which areas are wet, how wet they are, and how far the water has spread, with photos and readings.

Do you fix the pipe?

A plumber repairs the pipe. We find the wet area, dry it and repair the damage it caused.

Will you have to cut open my walls?

Usually only a small area, and only where readings confirm water. Many walls can be dried through small holes.

Can a hidden leak cause mold?

Yes. A slow leak inside a wall keeps drywall and wood damp for weeks, which is when mold grows out of sight. Finding it early keeps the repair small.

How long does leak detection take?

Most homes take one to a few hours, depending on how many areas need to be checked.

What is a slab leak?

A leak in a water line that runs under a concrete floor. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water, a damp carpet with no visible source and a meter that keeps moving.

Should I call a plumber or you first?

If you can see the leak, call a plumber to fix it and us to dry it, at the same time. If you only see damage and cannot find the source, call us first: our moisture map shows the plumber where to look.

Can a leak be in the main line outside my house?

Yes. If the meter moves but nothing inside is wet, the leak may be in the service line between the meter and your house. A plumber can confirm it.
