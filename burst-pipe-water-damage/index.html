Burst Pipe Water Damage Cleanup in Midvale

When a pipe bursts in your Midvale home, shut off the main water valve first, then call (385) 488-1110. Our crew removes the water, opens only what must be opened, and dries walls, floors and ceilings until moisture readings prove they are dry.

Quick answer: A burst pipe is an emergency because a small split can release hundreds of gallons a day into walls and subfloors. Mold can start in 24 to 48 hours. Fast extraction and professional drying keep the damage, and the repairs, small.

Pipe Burst in Your Midvale Home? Do This in the First 5 Minutes

  • Close the main shut-off valve. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Look in the basement or utility room near the water heater, or at the meter box near the street.
  • Open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the water left in the lines.
  • Turn off the water heater (gas to "pilot", or the breaker for electric).
  • Shut off power to wet rooms at the breaker, only if you can reach it without standing in water.
  • Photograph everything, then call us at (385) 488-1110.

Why Utah Pipes Burst When the Cold Snap Ends

Water expands by about 9% when it freezes. Ice inside a pipe builds pressure between the frozen section and the closed faucet, and the pipe splits there. The leak often stays hidden while the line is frozen. When temperatures climb, the ice melts and water pours out through the split. This is why many Salt Lake County homeowners find burst pipes in February, not January.

Where Pipes Burst in Midvale Homes

  • Exterior walls in homes built from the 1970s to the 1990s, where insulation is thin
  • Crawl spaces and unheated garages
  • Hose bibs (outdoor faucets) left connected to a garden hose
  • Under kitchen and bathroom sinks on outside walls
  • Sprinkler and service lines that were not blown out; the frost line in Salt Lake County is about 36 inches deep

Pipe type matters. Copper splits along a seam when it freezes. PEX flexes more, but its fittings can pull apart. Older galvanized steel in some mid-century homes corrodes from the inside, so it can fail at a joint even without a freeze.

Our Burst Pipe Cleanup Process in Midvale

  • Inspect and map moisture. Meters and a thermal camera show how far the water travelled inside walls and under floors.
  • Extract standing water from carpet, pad, hardwood and tile.
  • Remove what cannot be dried, such as soaked carpet pad or swollen baseboards. We cut drywall only where readings show it is needed.
  • Dry the structure with dehumidifiers, air movers and wall cavity drying. See how structural drying works.
  • Log readings every day until each material reaches its dry standard.
  • Repair and rebuild drywall, flooring and paint.

Plumber or Restoration Company: Who Do You Call First?

Call both, at the same time. The plumber fixes the pipe. We remove the water and dry the house. Waiting for the plumber before starting cleanup gives water more time to soak into drywall and wood.

How to Tell if a Pipe Burst Inside a Wall

  • A sudden jump in your water bill
  • Bubbling paint or a damp spot on drywall
  • Low water pressure at one fixture
  • A ceiling stain below a bathroom
  • Running water sounds when every tap is off

Quick meter test: turn off every faucet and water-using appliance, then look at your water meter. If the small leak indicator (a triangle or star dial) keeps turning, water is escaping somewhere in the house.

Does Utah Homeowners Insurance Cover a Burst Pipe?

Most homeowners policies cover water damage from a sudden pipe burst. They may deny claims if the home was left unheated or the leak was slow and ongoing. We give your adjuster photos, moisture maps and daily drying logs. Read more about insurance claim help.

Prevent the Next Burst Before Salt Lake County's Next Cold Snap

Insulate exposed pipes, disconnect garden hoses, keep the heat at 55°F or higher when you travel, and open sink cabinets on cold nights. Our winter guide covers every step: how to prevent frozen pipes.

Burst Pipe Water Damage FAQs

How much water can a burst pipe release?

A crack about 1/8 inch wide can leak around 250 gallons a day. A fully split line can release several gallons a minute.

Why did my pipe burst when it got warmer?

The pipe split while it was frozen. The ice held the water back until it thawed, then the leak started.

Do the walls have to be cut open after a burst pipe?

Not always. We measure moisture first and use wall cavity drying where we can. Drywall is removed only when it cannot be dried or is contaminated.

How long does drying take after a burst pipe?

Most jobs take 3 to 5 days. Hardwood floors and multi-room losses can take longer.

Can I dry it myself with fans?

Fans dry the surface, not the water trapped behind drywall and under floors. Hidden moisture is what leads to mold and warped floors.

Can I patch a burst pipe myself?

A repair clamp or self-fusing silicone tape can slow a small split until the plumber arrives. It is not a permanent fix, and it does nothing for the water already in your walls and floors.

A pipe burst while I was away. What should I do?

Shut the main valve, take photos and video before moving anything, check that the furnace is running, and call us. Water that sat for days may have turned into gray water and reached lower floors, so we test every room below the leak.

Is water from a burst pipe clean?

Water from a supply line starts as Category 1 clean water. After 24 to 48 hours, or once it runs through dirty materials, it is treated as Category 2 and needs antimicrobial cleaning.
