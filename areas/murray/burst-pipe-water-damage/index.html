Burst Pipe Water Damage in Murray, Utah

When a pipe bursts in your Murray home, shut off the main valve and call (385) 488-1110. Our crew is right next door in Midvale, and we extract the water, dry the structure with daily readings and repair the damage.

Quick answer: The median Murray home was built in the late 1970s, and many neighborhoods are older still. Pipes from those years, especially galvanized steel and older copper, corrode and fail at joints. A hard freeze, a pressure spike or simple age can split them.

Pipe Burst in Murray? First 5 Minutes

  • Close the main shut-off valve.
  • Turn off the water heater.
  • Shut off power to wet rooms from a dry spot.
  • Drain the lines from the lowest faucet.
  • Take photos and call (385) 488-1110.

Why Older Murray Pipes Fail

Pipe typeWhat goes wrong
Galvanized steelRusts from the inside, narrows and leaks at threaded joints
Older copperPinhole leaks and seams that split when frozen
Original shut-off valvesSeize or drip when finally turned
Old supply linesRubber hoses to toilets, sinks and washers crack with age

Homes along the creeks also tend to have crawl spaces and basements where pipes run in cool, damp areas that freeze first.

Murray Burst Pipe FAQs

How fast can you reach Murray for a burst pipe?

Murray borders Midvale, so we reach it fast. We send a crew as soon as you call, 24/7.

Should I replace my galvanized pipes after a burst?

If one section failed from corrosion, others are often close behind. Ask your plumber about repiping; we handle the drying and repairs.

Can hardwood floors in an older Murray home be saved?

Often, if drying starts quickly. We use floor drying mats and dry slowly to avoid cracking.

Does insurance cover a burst pipe in an older home?

Usually, for sudden bursts. Damage from long, slow leaks may not be covered.

Does Murray's hard water make pipes fail sooner?

It can. Hard water leaves mineral scale inside pipes, valves, water heaters and appliance connections, which adds wear over the years. A water softener and regular checks of older connections help.

What if a city water main break floods my Murray home?

Take photos, report it to the city right away, and call us to start removing water. A main break sends outside water across yards and into basements, so it is cleaned and sanitized, not just dried. Ask your insurer and the city how the claim will be handled.
