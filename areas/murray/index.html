Water Problems We See in Murray Homes

Creek-side groundwater and flooding

Little Cottonwood Creek runs through the middle of Murray, and Big Cottonwood Creek runs through its northeast side. During high runoff years, homes near the creek have seen water surround yards and push into basements. In many cases the water comes up from the ground rather than over the bank, which is why many creek-side homes run sump pumps. See flooded basement cleanup.

Summer storms

Monsoon storms have pushed Little Cottonwood Creek over barriers and flooded ground-floor units and basements in Murray. Storm water is outside water, so it is cleaned and sanitized during cleanup.

Older plumbing

The median Murray home was built in the late 1970s. Galvanized and copper supply lines, water heaters and clay sewer laterals from those years are now at the age where they fail. See burst pipe water damage in Murray and sewage cleanup.

Murray Water Damage FAQs

How fast can you reach Murray?

Murray borders Midvale, so it is one of the areas we reach fastest. We send a crew as soon as you call, 24/7.

Why does my Murray basement get wet near the creek?

During runoff, the ground near Little Cottonwood and Big Cottonwood Creeks fills with water, which pushes up into basements and crawl spaces.

Do you handle older Murray homes with galvanized pipes?

Yes. Older pipes fail at joints and corroded sections. We clean up and dry the damage while your plumber replaces the pipe.

Is creek or groundwater flooding covered by insurance?

Often not under a standard homeowners policy. Burst pipes usually are. See insurance claim help.

Do you serve businesses in Murray?

Yes, including offices, retail and apartment buildings.

Can plaster walls in older Murray homes be dried?

Often, yes. Plaster and lath hold more water than drywall and dry slower, so they need longer, targeted drying. Saving original plaster is usually better than tearing it out, and we check it with moisture readings every day.

Do you handle condos and townhomes in Murray?

Yes. In shared buildings near Fashion Place and along State Street, we trace water between units and document each one separately for owners and the HOA.
