Water Problems We See in Sandy Homes

Monsoon storms and overloaded storm drains

Sandy has seen powerful summer storms that city leaders described as 50- to 100-year rain events. Streets turned into rivers, manhole covers popped, window wells filled and water poured into basements. Storm water is treated as contaminated, so we clean and sanitize before drying. See flood damage restoration.

Window wells in finished basements

When a window well fills faster than its drain can empty it, water pours in around the basement window. In a finished basement, that soaks carpet, pad and the bottom of the drywall. See window well flooding and flooded basement cleanup in Sandy.

Spring runoff

Big snow years bring heavy runoff from the canyons. Sandy declared a flood-risk emergency in the record snow year of 2023, and saturated ground pushed water into basements across the area.

Winter burst pipes

Bench homes catch cold winds, and pipes on exterior walls freeze. See burst pipe water damage in Sandy.

Sandy Water Damage FAQs

How fast can you reach Sandy?

Sandy borders Midvale, so it is one of the areas we reach fastest. We send a crew as soon as you call, day or night.

Why did my Sandy basement flood in a summer storm?

Heavy storms can overwhelm storm drains and fill window wells in minutes. Water then pours in around basement windows or through the floor drain.

Can my finished basement be saved after storm flooding?

Often the framing and much of the structure can be saved. Carpet pad and drywall touched by storm water are usually replaced.

Is storm flooding covered by insurance in Sandy?

Surface water from storms is often excluded from standard policies. Check yours, and see insurance claim help.

Do you serve businesses in Sandy?

Yes, including offices and retail around South Towne.

Do you serve White City, Granite and Alta Vista?

Yes. Along with the city of Sandy, we cover nearby White City, Granite and the Alta Vista area, including homes on the bench below Little Cottonwood Canyon.

Should I clear the storm drain on my Sandy street?

If it is safe, yes. Leaves, snow and trash on the grate are a common reason streets flood during summer cloudbursts. A clear drain keeps water out of window wells up and down the block.
