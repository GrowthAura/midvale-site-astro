Flooded Basement Cleanup in Sandy, Utah

When a storm, a flooded window well or a failed pump puts water in your Sandy basement, our Midvale crew pumps it out, removes what cannot be saved, sanitizes and dries the space, and rebuilds your finished basement. Call (385) 488-1110.

Quick answer: Most Sandy basement floods start outside: heavy summer storms that overwhelm storm drains, window wells that fill faster than they drain, or snowmelt soaking the ground in spring. Outside water is treated as contaminated, so carpet pad and wet drywall in finished basements are usually replaced.

How Sandy Basements Flood

CauseWhat happens in Sandy
Monsoon stormsStreets and storm drains overflow, water reaches doors and window wells
Window wellsWells fill and water pours in around basement windows
Spring runoffSaturated ground pushes water through walls and floor joints
Sump pump failurePump quits during a storm or power outage
Burst pipe or water heaterClean water from inside the home

During a recent summer storm that city leaders called a 50- to 100-year event, Sandy residents reported window wells filling, windows breaking and basements taking on water.

Our Sandy Basement Cleanup Process

  • Pump out standing water with submersible pumps.
  • Remove wet carpet pad, baseboards and drywall above the water line.
  • Clean and sanitize the floor, framing and walls.
  • Dry the slab and wall cavities with dehumidifiers and air movers.
  • Check readings daily until dry.
  • Rebuild drywall, carpet and trim.

See our full guide to flooded basement cleanup.

Protect Your Sandy Basement Before the Next Storm

  • Add clear covers to basement window wells
  • Test that window well drains empty within minutes
  • Keep street storm drains in front of your home clear of leaves and debris
  • Extend downspouts away from the foundation
  • Add a battery backup to your sump pump

Sandy Basement Flooding FAQs

How fast can you reach a flooded basement in Sandy?

Sandy borders Midvale. We send a crew with pumps as soon as you call, day or night.

Is storm water in my basement dangerous?

Storm water is treated as contaminated because it can carry soil, bacteria and chemicals. Keep children and pets out until it is cleaned.

Can my finished basement be saved?

The framing and much of the structure usually can. Carpet pad and drywall touched by storm water are usually replaced.

Does insurance cover storm flooding in a Sandy basement?

Surface water is often excluded from standard policies. Sump overflow or water backup may need an add-on. Burst pipes are usually covered.

Can a garden hose left running in a window well flood the basement?

Yes, and it happens more than you would think. A hose or sprinkler aimed at a window well can fill it overnight and push water in around the window. Water from a hose is clean, so this kind of flood is often easier to dry than storm water.

Do you clean root cellars and cold storage rooms?

Yes. Many older Sandy homes have a root cellar or cold room under the porch. They flood easily and dry slowly, so we pump them out, clean them and dry the concrete before anything goes back in.
