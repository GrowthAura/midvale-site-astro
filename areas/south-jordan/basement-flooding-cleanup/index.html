Flooded Basement Cleanup in South Jordan, Utah

When your finished South Jordan basement takes on water, from a failed sump pump, groundwater, a storm or a burst line, our Midvale crew pumps it out, dries the slab and walls, and rebuilds the space. Call (385) 488-1110.

Quick answer: Many South Jordan homes have finished basements over a sump pit. When the pump fails during spring runoff or a heavy storm, water rises into carpet and drywall within hours. Homes along 1300 West and near the Jordan River can also see higher groundwater after wet winters.

Our South Jordan Basement Cleanup Process

  • Pump out standing water and get the sump pit working again.
  • Remove wet carpet pad, baseboards and drywall that cannot be dried.
  • Clean and treat floors and walls; sump and groundwater are treated as gray or outside water.
  • Dry the slab and wall cavities.
  • Check readings daily until dry.
  • Rebuild walls, carpet and trim.

See our full guide: flooded basement cleanup.

Protect Your South Jordan Basement

  • Test the sump pump before spring by pouring water into the pit
  • Add a battery backup pump and a high-water alarm
  • Replace pumps older than about 7 to 10 years
  • Keep window wells covered and drains clear
  • Add leak sensors near the sump pit and water heater

South Jordan Basement Flooding FAQs

How fast can you reach South Jordan?

South Jordan is a short drive from our Midvale base. We send a crew with pumps as soon as you call, 24/7.

My sump pump failed. Can the finished basement be saved?

Often the framing and much of the drywall can be saved if we start fast. Carpet pad is usually replaced.

How long do sump pumps last?

Many last about 7 to 10 years. Test yours each spring and replace it before it fails.

Does insurance cover a sump pump overflow?

Often only with a water backup or sump overflow add-on to the policy.

My basement bathroom ejector pump failed. Is that sewage?

Yes. A sewage ejector pump lifts waste from a basement bathroom or laundry up to the main sewer line. If it fails, the overflow is Category 3 black water and needs full sewage cleanup.

Should I buy flood insurance in South Jordan?

South Jordan City suggests residents consider flood insurance as part of preparing for spring storms and high groundwater. Standard homeowners policies usually leave out outside flooding, so ask your agent what fits your home.
