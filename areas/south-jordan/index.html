Water Problems We See in South Jordan Homes

Finished basements and sump pumps

Many South Jordan homes have sump pits under finished basements. When a pump fails during spring runoff or a heavy storm, water rises into carpet and drywall fast. See flooded basement cleanup in South Jordan.

Groundwater near the Jordan River

South Jordan City notes that while most of the city is not prone to high groundwater, neighborhoods along 1300 West and close to the Jordan River can see high groundwater after wet winters. The city recommends checking sump pumps in those areas.

Upstairs laundry and appliance leaks

Newer two-story homes often put the washer upstairs. A burst hose sends water through the floor into the ceiling and walls below and.

Builder plumbing

PEX lines and fittings in newer homes can pull apart or leak at crimp fittings, often inside walls or ceilings where they are hard to see.

South Jordan Water Damage FAQs

How fast can you reach South Jordan?

South Jordan is a short drive from our Midvale base. We send a crew as soon as you call, day or night.

My sump pump failed and the basement flooded. What now?

Stay out of the water if outlets are near it, unplug what you safely can, take photos and call. Sump water is treated as gray water and needs cleaning and drying.

Is groundwater a problem in Daybreak?

Most of South Jordan is not prone to high groundwater, but lower areas and homes near the Jordan River can see it in wet years. A working sump pump with battery backup is the best protection.

Can a washer leak upstairs damage the ceiling below?

Yes. Water travels through the floor and shows up as stains or a sagging ceiling below. We dry both floors.

Does insurance cover sump pump failure?

It depends on the policy. Many need a water backup or sump overflow add-on.

Do you serve Glenmoor and the older east-side neighborhoods?

Yes. We cover Glenmoor, the neighborhoods along the Jordan River Parkway and 1300 West, and the newer areas like Daybreak to the west.

Are newer South Jordan homes less likely to have water damage?

Not really, just different damage. Newer homes have fewer old pipe failures, but finished basements, upstairs laundry rooms and PEX fittings mean a single leak can reach a lot of drywall and carpet fast.
