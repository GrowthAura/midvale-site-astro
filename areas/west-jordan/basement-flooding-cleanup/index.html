Flooded Basement Cleanup in West Jordan, Utah

When groundwater, storm water or a failed sump pump floods your West Jordan basement, our Midvale crew pumps it out, cleans and sanitizes, dries the slab and walls, and rebuilds the finished space. Call (385) 488-1110.

Quick answer: In West Jordan, basement water usually comes from one of three places: rising groundwater in lower areas near the Jordan River and Bingham Creek during high-water years, summer thunderstorms that flood streets and yards, or a sump pump that fails when it is needed most.

Why West Jordan Basements Flood

CauseWhere and when
GroundwaterLower areas near the Jordan River and Bingham Creek, during spring runoff and high river years
ThunderstormsNeighborhoods where streets and yards flood, such as near 7800 South and 6700 West
Sump pump failureHomes that rely on pumps during runoff or heavy storms
Window wellsFinished basements with uncovered wells
Inside sourcesBurst pipes and water heaters

In high-water years, the Jordan River has run high enough to close the river trail under 7800 South for weeks, and ground near the river can stay wet for a long time.

Our West Jordan Basement Cleanup Process

  • Pump out standing water and check the sump pit.
  • Remove wet carpet pad, baseboards and drywall above the water line.
  • Clean and sanitize floors, framing and walls.
  • Dry the slab and walls with dehumidifiers and air movers.
  • Check readings daily until dry.
  • Rebuild walls, flooring and trim.

Protect Your West Jordan Basement

  • Test your sump pump before spring and add a battery backup
  • Keep the sump discharge line clear and pointed away from the house
  • Cover window wells and keep their drains clear
  • Grade soil away from the foundation

West Jordan Basement Flooding FAQs

How fast can you reach a flooded basement in West Jordan?

West Jordan is just across the river from Midvale. We send a crew with pumps as soon as you call, 24/7.

Why does groundwater come into my basement in spring?

In wet years, the ground near the Jordan River and Bingham Creek fills with water, which pushes up through the floor and the base of walls.

My sump pump keeps running. Is that a problem?

It can mean high groundwater, a stuck float or an undersized pump. Have it checked before it fails.

Does insurance cover groundwater in my basement?

Usually not under a standard homeowners policy. Burst pipes and water heater failures usually are.

Will an interior drain system stop groundwater in my basement?

For many homes, yes. A drain along the inside of the foundation walls carries groundwater to a sump pit, and the pump sends it outside before it reaches carpet and drywall. A waterproofing contractor can tell you if your home needs one.

Can the Jordan River flood my West Jordan basement?

The river rarely flows straight into homes, but in high-water years it raises the groundwater in lower areas nearby. That water comes up through the slab and the base of the walls, which is why sump pumps matter near the river.
