West Jordan Water Damage FAQs

How fast can you reach West Jordan?

West Jordan is just across the Jordan River from Midvale. We send a crew as soon as you call, day or night.

Why does my West Jordan basement get water in spring?

In high-water years, groundwater can rise in lower areas near the Jordan River and Bingham Creek and push into basements.

My sump pump failed. What now?

Stop using water, keep out of the wet area if outlets are near it, and call. Sump water is treated as gray water and needs cleaning and drying.

Is groundwater flooding covered by insurance?

Usually not under a standard homeowners policy. Burst pipes and water heater failures usually are.

Do you serve businesses in West Jordan?

Yes, including retail around Jordan Landing and offices along Bangerter Highway.

Can a slow water heater drip damage a West Jordan basement?

Yes. A small drip from the tank or valve can soak carpet pad and the bottom of the drywall for days before anyone notices damp carpet. Check around the heater every few months, and call if the carpet feels wet.

Do you serve the older neighborhoods near Gardner Village?

Yes. We cover the older homes near Gardner Village and the Jordan River as well as the newer subdivisions west of Bangerter Highway.
