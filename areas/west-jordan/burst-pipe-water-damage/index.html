Burst Pipe Water Damage in West Jordan, Utah

When a pipe bursts in your West Jordan home, shut off the main water valve and call (385) 488-1110. Our crew is just across the Jordan River in Midvale, and we remove the water, dry walls and floors with daily readings, and repair the damage.

Quick answer: West Jordan has two kinds of burst pipe calls. Older homes near the river and Gardner Village have aging copper and galvanized lines that split in a hard freeze. Newer west-side homes have PEX lines that resist freezing better but can fail at fittings, often inside walls or ceilings.

Pipe Burst in West Jordan? First 5 Minutes

  • Close the main shut-off valve.
  • Turn off the water heater.
  • Shut off power to wet rooms from a dry spot.
  • Drain the lines from the lowest faucet.
  • Take photos and call (385) 488-1110.

West Jordan Burst Pipe FAQs

How fast can you reach West Jordan for a burst pipe?

West Jordan is just across the river from Midvale. We send a crew as soon as you call, 24/7.

Can PEX pipes burst?

PEX handles freezing better than copper, but fittings can still crack or pull apart, and lines in very cold spots can split.

Why did my pipe burst after the cold snap ended?

The pipe split while frozen, and the leak started when the ice melted.

Does insurance cover a burst pipe in West Jordan?

Usually, if the burst was sudden and the home was heated.

Does insurance pay to fix the pipe itself?

Usually not. Policies tend to cover the water damage from a sudden burst, like drywall, flooring and belongings, while the pipe repair is treated as maintenance. Ask your adjuster how your policy handles it.

Can a frozen outdoor faucet flood the inside of my West Jordan home?

Yes. When a hose bib freezes, the split is often inside the wall, behind the faucet. The leak shows up in the basement or the room behind it when you turn the hose on in spring.
