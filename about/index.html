About Our Midvale Water Damage Team

Water Damage Restoration Midvale is a local restoration team based in Midvale, Utah. We remove water, dry homes and businesses to a measured dry standard, stop mold, and rebuild what was damaged, for families and property owners across Midvale and nearby Salt Lake County cities.

(385) 488-1110

Who We Are

We started this company to give Midvale homeowners one local team they can call at 2 a.m. when a pipe bursts, and still count on two weeks later when the last wall is painted.

We live and work in the Salt Lake Valley, so we know the water problems homes here face: frozen pipes in January, burst lines in the February thaw, ice dams, spring snowmelt in basements, monsoon storms and rooftop swamp coolers.

How We Work

  • Answer fast, day or night. A real person picks up (385) 488-1110, 24/7.
  • Measure, don't guess. Moisture meters, thermal imaging and daily readings decide what is wet and when it is dry.
  • Save what can be saved. We dry before we tear out.
  • Explain everything. You get photos, moisture maps and a written plan.
  • Finish the job. The same team that dries your home rebuilds it.

Our Training and Standards

Our technicians are trained in water damage restoration, applied structural drying and mold remediation, and we follow the IICRC S500 water damage standard and the IICRC S520 mold standard on every job.

  • We tell you what we found, in plain words, before we start
  • We remove only what readings show cannot be saved

About Us FAQs

Are you a local company?

Yes. We are based in Midvale and work across the south and central Salt Lake Valley.

Who will come to my home?

A member of our own crew, trained in water damage restoration. We tell you who is coming before we arrive.

Do you handle the repairs too?

Yes. We dry your home and then rebuild it, so you work with one team.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes. We document every job for your adjuster.

Are you open on holidays?

Yes. Water damage does not wait for business hours, so we answer (385) 488-1110 every day of the year.

Do you handle small jobs?

Yes. A leak under one sink or a single wet room gets the same care and documentation as a flooded basement.

What happens when your crew arrives?

We introduce ourselves, put on floor covers, check that the area is safe, and walk the damage with you. Before any work starts, you hear what we found and what we plan to do.

What if I am not sure the damage is serious?

Call and describe it, or send photos. If a towel and a fan will do, we will tell you. If water has reached inside walls or under floors, we will explain why it needs drying.
