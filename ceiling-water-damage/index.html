Ceiling Water Damage Repair in Midvale

A stained, dripping or sagging ceiling means water is sitting above it. Our Midvale crew stops the source, drains and dries the ceiling and the space above it, and repairs the drywall, texture and paint so it matches the rest of the room. Call (385) 488-1110.

(385) 488-1110

Quick answer: Wet drywall cannot hold its own weight, so a sagging ceiling can fall without warning. Keep people out of the room, put a bucket under the low spot, and call. A dry brown stain can often be sealed and painted; a soft, sagging or crumbling ceiling needs drying and new drywall.

Sagging or Dripping Ceiling? Safety Steps First

  • Keep everyone out from under the sag, including pets.
  • Turn off the light fixture and fan in that ceiling at the breaker. Water and wiring do not mix.
  • Stop the source if you can: shut the main water valve, or the water to an upstairs toilet, tub or swamp cooler.
  • Put a bucket and tarp under the drip and move furniture away.
  • Call (385) 488-1110. We can safely release trapped water so the ceiling does not collapse.

The stain is not always right under the leak. Water runs along joists and pipes before it shows. We trace it with moisture meters and a thermal camera.

Popcorn Ceilings in Older Midvale Homes

Many Midvale homes were built in the 1970s and early 1980s, when textured popcorn ceilings were common. Some of that texture contains asbestos. If your home was built before about 1980, the texture should be tested before anyone cuts or scrapes a wet ceiling.

Drying vs Replacing a Water-Damaged Ceiling

ConditionWhat we usually do

Damp but firmDry in place, then seal and paint
Soft, sagging or crumblingRelease water, remove the damaged section, dry above, install new drywall
Mold on the back of drywallContain, remove and treat

We confirm dry with a moisture meter, not by touch, before any patching or paint.

Our Ceiling Water Damage Process in Midvale

What Affects How Long Ceiling Repair Takes

  • How fast the source is found and fixed by the plumber or roofer
  • How big the wet area is and whether the attic insulation is soaked
  • Drying above the ceiling: wet joists and insulation must dry before new drywall goes up
  • Texture matching: knockdown or popcorn texture adds dry time between coats
  • Asbestos testing for older textured ceilings, if needed

Ceiling Water Damage FAQs

Should I poke a hole in a sagging ceiling?

Only if it is bulging with water, the power to that ceiling is off, and you can stand to the side. Put a bucket underneath first. If you are unsure, keep out of the room and call us.

Will a water stain on the ceiling go away?

No. Once the area is dry, a stain-blocking primer and paint cover it. Regular paint lets the stain bleed through.

Can a wet ceiling collapse?

Yes. Soaked drywall loses its strength and can fall, especially when water pools above it.

Who fixes a ceiling leak, a plumber or a restoration company?

The plumber or roofer fixes the source. We dry the ceiling and attic and repair the drywall. Call both at once.

How long does ceiling repair take?

Drying usually takes 2 to 4 days. Drywall, texture and paint add a few more days.

Can I paint over a water stain on my ceiling?

Only after the area is fully dry and the leak is fixed. Use a stain-blocking primer first; regular paint lets the stain bleed back through.

Why does my ceiling only leak when it rains or snows?

That usually points to the roof: flashing, a vent boot, a swamp cooler curb or an ice dam. A plumbing leak usually drips no matter the weather.

Does a ceiling stain mean there is mold above it?

Not always, but a stain that keeps coming back or smells musty often means the drywall, insulation or joists above stayed wet. We check with a moisture meter, and a small inspection hole tells us the rest.

I rent or live in a condo. What should I do about a ceiling leak?

Tell your landlord or HOA in writing right away, with photos and the time you noticed it. If water is coming from a unit above, they need to stop it there. We work with owners, managers and HOAs to dry and repair the ceiling.

Can you match my ceiling texture?

Yes. We match knockdown, orange peel and smooth finishes so the repair blends in. Popcorn texture is tested first in homes built before about 1980.
