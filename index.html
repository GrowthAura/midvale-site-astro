Water Damage in Midvale Right Now? Do These 4 Things First

  • Stop the water. Turn the main shut-off valve clockwise. In most Midvale homes it sits in the basement or utility room near the water heater.
  • Cut the power to wet rooms. Switch them off at the breaker only if you can reach it without standing in water.
  • Take photos and video of every wet room before you move anything. Your insurance claim starts here.
  • Call (385) 488-1110. The longer water sits, the further it wicks into drywall, wood and carpet pad.

Salt Lake Valley Water Problems Most Companies Miss

Salt Lake Valley homes have water risks you will not read about on national websites. We handle each one as its own service.

  • Swamp cooler leaks: a stuck float valve or a frozen supply line sends water through the roof and into the ceiling under the cooler.
  • Window well flooding: snow and rain collect in basement window wells, then pour in around the window frame.
  • Ice dam leaks: melted snow refreezes at the roof edge and backs up under the shingles into walls and ceilings.
  • Fire sprinkler discharge: one sprinkler head can release about 20 gallons a minute of dirty, stagnant water.

Water Damage Restoration FAQs for Midvale Homeowners

How fast can you get to my home in Midvale?

We send a crew as soon as you call, 24/7. Midvale is our home base, so it is the area we reach fastest.

What should I do while I wait for you?

Shut off the main water valve, turn off power to wet rooms if it is safe, and take photos. Move small furniture and rugs off wet floors.

How long does it take to dry a house after water damage?

Most rooms dry in 3 to 5 days with professional equipment. We confirm dry with moisture readings, not by touch.

Do you work with my insurance company?

Yes. We document the loss in detail and can talk with your adjuster. You choose who restores your home, not the insurance company.

Will I get mold after water damage?

Mold can start growing within 24 to 48 hours on wet materials. Fast extraction and drying is the best way to stop it.

Is it safe to stay in my house?

Usually, for clean water in one or two rooms. Sewage, flood water or a sagging wet ceiling are reasons to keep people out of that area.

Do you also do the repairs?

Yes. After drying, we replace drywall, flooring and trim, so you deal with one team from start to finish.

What does a water damage restoration company do?

It stops further damage and returns your home to how it was. That means removing the water, drying the structure to measured readings, cleaning and preventing mold, and rebuilding drywall, floors and trim.

What is the difference between water mitigation and restoration?

Mitigation is the emergency part: extraction, removing what cannot be saved and drying. Restoration is the rebuild that follows. We handle both, so there is no gap between the two.

Do you handle storm damage and roof leaks?

Yes. After a monsoon cloudburst or a heavy snowmelt, we dry the attic, ceilings and basement, and protect open areas until roof repairs are done.
