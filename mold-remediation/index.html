Mold Remediation in Midvale After Water Damage

Our Midvale crew removes mold the safe way: we seal off the area, filter the air with HEPA machines, remove or clean affected materials, and dry the moisture source so mold does not come back. Call (385) 488-1110.

Quick answer: Mold needs water. It can start growing on wet drywall, wood and carpet within 24 to 48 hours. Removing mold without fixing the leak or drying the structure only buys time. Real remediation does both.

Signs of Mold After Water Damage

  • A musty, earthy smell, often worst in basements and closets
  • Black, green or white spots on drywall, baseboards or ceilings
  • Stains or bubbling paint where there was a leak
  • Warped baseboards or soft drywall
  • Allergy-like symptoms that ease when you leave the house

Small spots under about 10 square feet can sometimes be cleaned by a homeowner. Larger growth, mold inside walls, or mold after sewage or flood water needs professional removal.

Why Mold Grows in Midvale Homes, Even in Dry Utah

Many people think Utah's dry air stops mold. It does not, once a material stays wet. In Midvale and the rest of the Salt Lake Valley, we see mold most often from:

Swamp cooler leaks soaking attic insulation and ceilings Spring snowmelt pushing moisture into basement walls Winter freeze-thaw and ice dams leaking into walls Slow plumbing leaks behind walls and under sinks
  • Bathrooms in older 1970s to 1990s homes with weak exhaust fans

Our Mold Remediation Process in Midvale

We follow the IICRC S520 standard for professional mold remediation and EPA guidance.

  • Inspect and find the moisture source. Meters and a thermal camera show where water is feeding the mold.
  • Contain the area with plastic barriers and negative air pressure so spores do not spread to clean rooms.
  • Filter the air with HEPA air scrubbers during the work.
  • Remove moldy drywall, insulation and carpet that cannot be cleaned.
  • Clean framing and hard surfaces with HEPA vacuuming and antimicrobial treatment.
  • Dry the structure to a dry standard.
  • Check the result. We confirm surfaces are clean and dry, and can arrange third-party air testing if you want proof before rebuilding.
  • Rebuild walls and finishes.

How to Keep Mold From Coming Back

  • Fix leaks fast and dry wet areas within 24 to 48 hours
  • Keep indoor humidity below about 50%
  • Run bathroom fans during and 20 minutes after showers
  • Winterize swamp coolers before the first freeze
  • Keep window wells clear and gutters flowing

Mold Remediation FAQs

How fast does mold grow after a leak?

Mold can start in 24 to 48 hours on wet materials. Visible growth often shows up within a week or two.

Does insurance cover mold removal?

Mold caused by a sudden, covered water event, such as a burst pipe or appliance failure, is often covered, sometimes up to a set limit. Mold from a slow leak or long-term humidity usually is not. We document the water source and the damage for your adjuster.

Can a house with mold be saved?

Almost always. Mold-damaged drywall, insulation and carpet are replaced, and framing and hard surfaces are cleaned. Once the moisture is fixed, the home is safe to rebuild.

Can mold grow in the attic or inside walls without showing?

Yes. Swamp cooler leaks, ice dams and slow pipe leaks often grow mold on the back of drywall or the roof deck first. A musty smell without visible spots is a common sign.

Is black mold dangerous?

Any mold can trigger allergies and breathing problems, especially for children, older adults and people with asthma. Color alone does not tell you how harmful it is.

Can I just spray bleach on mold?

Bleach can lighten stains on hard surfaces but does not reach roots inside drywall or wood. It also does nothing about the moisture causing the mold.

Do I need a mold test?

If mold is visible, testing is often not needed to start cleanup. Testing helps when you smell mold but cannot find it, or when you want proof after remediation.

Can I stay home during mold remediation?

Usually yes. Containment and negative air keep the work area sealed from the rest of the house.
