Once your home is dry, our Midvale crew puts it back together: new drywall, matched texture and paint, flooring, baseboards, cabinets and trim. The same team that dried your home rebuilds it, so nothing gets lost between companies. Call (385) 488-1110.

Quick answer: Repairs should start only after moisture readings prove the structure is dry. Closing up a wall or laying new flooring over damp materials traps water inside and leads to mold, warping and repeat repairs.

From Dry-Out to a Finished Room

  • Dry and verify: every wet material reaches its dry standard.
  • Scope the repairs: we measure and list every item that needs to be replaced.
  • Rebuild: insulation, drywall, texture, primer and paint.
  • Finish: flooring, baseboards, trim, cabinets and fixtures.
  • Walk-through: we check the work with you room by room.

Matching Older Midvale Homes

Much of Midvale was built between 1970 and 1999. We match the textures used in those homes, like orange peel and knockdown walls and textured ceilings, so a repair blends in. Water Damage Repair FAQs

Do you do both the cleanup and the repairs?

Yes. We remove the water, dry the structure and then rebuild, so you deal with one team.

Can you match my wall and ceiling texture?

Yes. We match common textures like orange peel, knockdown and smooth, and blend paint to the rest of the room.

How long do repairs take after water damage?

A single room often takes 3 to 7 days after drying. A finished basement or several rooms can take a few weeks.

Will you work with my insurance adjuster?

Yes. We share photos, moisture readings and a line-by-line repair scope with your adjuster.

Do I need a permit for water damage repairs in Midvale?

Simple drywall and flooring work often does not. Electrical, plumbing or structural changes may need a Midvale City permit. We tell you before we start.

Can I stay in my home during repairs?

Most families do. We close off the work area, keep dust down and keep walkways clear. Kitchen or bathroom repairs may mean planning around that room for a few days.

Do you replace the whole wall or just the damaged part?

Usually just the damaged part. We cut out the wet section, replace it and blend the texture and paint so the repair does not show.

What if the subfloor or framing has rotted?

Soft, rotted wood is cut out and replaced with new lumber or subfloor sheathing, then the area is dried and sealed before flooring goes back down. Long, slow leaks under sinks, toilets and tubs are the usual cause.

Can you match flooring that is no longer made?

We try to match it first. If that is not possible, options include borrowing boards from a closet, using a transition strip, or replacing a whole connected area so it looks uniform. We go over the choices with you and your adjuster.

Do you handle cabinets and countertops?

Yes. Swollen cabinet bases and toe kicks are replaced, and countertops are lifted and reset when needed, so the kitchen or bathroom goes back to how it was.
