Swamp Cooler Water Damage Cleanup in Midvale

A leaking swamp cooler sends water through the roof opening and into your attic and ceiling, usually right around the cooler vent. Our Midvale crew finds the leak path, removes the wet insulation and drywall that cannot be saved, and dries the ceiling and attic to prevent mold. Call (385) 488-1110.

(385) 488-1110

Quick answer: Most swamp cooler leaks come from a stuck float valve, a rusted or cracked drain pan, a clogged overflow, or a supply line that froze because the cooler was not winterized. Turn off the water to the cooler first, then call for drying before the ceiling sags.

Swamp Cooler Leaking Into the Ceiling? Do This Now

  • Turn off the cooler and its water supply. The small supply line usually has a valve in the attic, under a sink or at the water heater.
  • Put a bucket under the drip and move furniture out of the way.
  • Do not stand under a sagging ceiling. If it bulges, keep people and pets out of the room.
  • Take photos of the stain, the vent and the cooler on the roof.
  • Call (385) 488-1110. Wet attic insulation holds water against the drywall for days.

How Swamp Coolers Leak on Salt Lake Valley Roofs

An evaporative cooler sits on the roof with a water reservoir, pads and a pump. When any part fails, water has a straight path down through the roof curb and duct into your home.

PartWhat goes wrongWhat you notice
Float valveSticks open, reservoir overfillsWater running off the roof, drip at the vent
Drain pan / reservoirRust holes or cracksSlow, steady ceiling stain
Overflow / bleed lineClogged with mineral scaleOverflow spills onto the roof deck
Supply lineFreezes and splits when not winterizedLeak after the first hard freeze in fall
Roof curb sealCracked sealant around the cooler baseLeak during rain or snowmelt, even with the cooler off

Utah's hard water speeds up mineral scale and rust in cooler pans, floats and bleed lines. A pan that has rusted through should not be run again until it is replaced. To find a leak on the roof, run the pump with the fan off: any water you see is coming from the water system, not blown out by the fan.

Hidden Attic and Ceiling Damage From a Leaking Cooler

  • Wet attic insulation that holds water against the ceiling
  • Soaked ceiling drywall around the vent and in nearby rooms
  • Ductwork carrying water to other vents
  • Roof decking that stayed wet under the cooler
  • Mold on the back of drywall and on framing

We use a thermal camera and moisture meters to follow the water from the roof to the room, so we remove only what is wet.

Why the stain lies: cooler water usually spreads sideways across the top of the ceiling drywall and through the insulation before it shows below. The wet area is often two or three times larger than the stain. Any brown ring or bubbling paint under a cooler vent is worth a moisture check, even if it looks dry to the touch.

Stop It Before Utah's First Hard Freeze

  • Winterize every fall, before the first hard freeze. Shut off and drain the supply line, drain the reservoir, and cover the cooler. Step-by-step guide: how to winterize a swamp cooler.
  • Check the float at the start of each summer.
  • Replace rusted pans instead of patching them.
  • Reseal the roof curb if you see cracked sealant.

Swamp Cooler Leak FAQs

Why is my swamp cooler leaking into the house?

The most common causes are a stuck float valve, a rusted drain pan, a clogged overflow, a frozen supply line or a failed roof seal.

Can a swamp cooler leak cause mold?

Yes. Water trapped in attic insulation and ceiling drywall can grow mold within 24 to 48 hours. Utah's dry air does not stop it once materials stay wet.

When should I winterize my swamp cooler in Utah?

Before the first hard freeze, usually in October. A supply line left full can freeze and split.

My cooler is off. Why is the ceiling still leaking?

The roof seal around the cooler can fail. Rain and snowmelt then run down the cooler duct into the ceiling.

Do I need to replace the whole ceiling?

Usually not. We dry what can be dried and replace only the drywall that is soaked, sagging or growing mold.

How long can a swamp cooler leak go unnoticed?

Days or even weeks. The attic is out of sight, and a slow drip from a rusted pan soaks the insulation before it reaches the ceiling paint. By the time the stain appears, mold may already be growing above it.

Is swamp cooler water clean water?

It starts as clean tap water, but water that sits warm in the reservoir collects minerals, dust and bacteria. When it has soaked insulation for a day or more, we treat it as Category 2 water and clean the framing with antimicrobial before we rebuild.

Can I keep running the cooler until the repair?

No. Turn the water off at the cooler supply valve. Running it with a stuck float or a rusted pan keeps feeding water into the attic.
