Commercial Water Damage Restoration in Midvale

When water hits an office, store, restaurant or apartment building, every closed hour costs you. Our Midvale crew responds 24/7, sets up drying zones so unaffected areas can stay open, and documents everything for owners, property managers and adjusters. Call (385) 488-1110.

(385) 488-1110

Quick answer: Commercial water damage is handled the same way as residential, but faster and at a larger scale: stop the source, extract, dry with more equipment, and keep the business running where it is safe. Clear photos, readings and reports matter even more, because more people need to sign off.

Water in Your Building? First Steps for Owners and Managers

  • Stop the water at the fixture, riser or main valve. For a sprinkler discharge, call your fire protection company.
  • Shut off power to wet areas if it is safe.
  • Move people and inventory out of the wet area.
  • Notify tenants whose units may be affected, including floors below.
  • Photograph everything and call (385) 488-1110.

What Affects How Long Commercial Restoration Takes

  • Building size and how many floors or units are wet
  • Water type: sewage or sprinkler water adds cleaning and removal
  • Materials: carpet tile, ceiling tile, drywall and concrete dry at different rates
  • Business hours: after-hours work protects operations but spreads work over more days
  • Sign-offs from owners, tenants and adjusters

Commercial Water Damage FAQs

Can you work after business hours?

Yes. Extraction, setup and repairs can be scheduled at night or on weekends to reduce disruption.

Do you handle apartment and condo buildings?

Yes. We trace water between units, dry each one and document each unit separately for owners and tenants.

How do you document a commercial loss for insurance?

We provide photos, moisture maps, daily drying logs and a unit-by-unit repair scope.

Can my business stay open while you dry?

Often, yes. We contain the wet areas so other parts of the building can be used safely.

Do you handle sprinkler discharges?

Yes. See fire sprinkler water cleanup.

What should a property manager do first after a leak in a unit?

Shut off water to the unit or riser, check the units below, tell affected tenants, take photos and call for extraction. Water spreads between units fast.

Do you work with our building's insurance carrier?

Yes. We share photos, moisture maps, drying logs and a unit-by-unit scope with every carrier involved.

Can you help with a business interruption claim?

We do not file the claim, but our dated photos, drying logs and timeline show when areas were closed and when they were usable again. Adjusters use that record for business interruption coverage.

Do you protect inventory, records and equipment?

Yes. We move or block up stock, files and electronics, photograph and list anything wet, and keep dry items out of the work zone so they stay that way.

Do you handle snowmelt and storm water at commercial entrances?

Yes. Spring snowmelt and summer storms often push water in at loading docks, storefronts and lower levels. We extract it, clean the entry area and dry the floor and walls.
