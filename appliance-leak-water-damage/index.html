Appliance Leak Water Damage Cleanup in Midvale

Washing machines, dishwashers, refrigerator ice makers and AC drains leak slowly and quietly, so the damage usually hides under the floor and inside cabinets before anyone sees it. Quick answer: Turn off the water valve behind the appliance and unplug it. Then check the floor next to it: soft spots, lifting laminate or a musty smell mean water has reached the subfloor, and it needs professional drying.

Stop the Leak: First Steps

  • Close the water valve behind the appliance, or the main shut-off.
  • Unplug it if you can reach the plug from a dry spot.
  • Pull it out carefully to check behind it, if it is safe to move.
  • Mop up surface water and take photos.
  • Call (385) 488-1110 so we can check the subfloor and walls.

Hidden Damage Under Cabinets and Floors

  • Subfloor soaks up water and swells or softens
  • Laminate and vinyl plank trap water underneath and start to lift at the seams
  • Cabinet bases made of particle board swell and crumble
  • Drywall wicks water up from the floor
  • Ceilings below stain or sag if the appliance is upstairs

We find it with moisture meters and a thermal camera before anything is torn out.

Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Midvale

  • Map moisture under floors, inside cabinets and in walls.
  • Extract water and remove toe kicks or baseboards to reach hidden water.
  • Dry cabinets, subfloors and walls in place where possible.
  • Replace materials that cannot be saved, like swollen particle board.

What Affects How Long Appliance Leak Cleanup Takes

  • How long the leak ran before anyone noticed
  • Flooring type: vinyl plank can often be lifted and reused; laminate usually cannot
  • Cabinets: particle board bases may need replacement
  • Upstairs or main floor: upstairs leaks can reach ceilings and walls below
  • Subfloor condition: soft or swollen subfloor adds repair time

Prevent the Next Appliance Leak

  • Replace rubber washer hoses with braided steel every 5 years
  • Leave 4 inches behind the washer so hoses do not kink
  • Check the ice maker line when you clean behind the fridge
  • Put a drain pan under the washer if it is upstairs
  • Add water leak sensors near the washer, dishwasher and fridge

Appliance Leak FAQs

How do I know if my dishwasher damaged the subfloor?

Look for soft or spongy floor in front of the dishwasher, lifting flooring, or a musty smell under the sink. A moisture meter confirms it.

How often should washing machine hoses be replaced?

About every 5 years, or right away if you see bulges or cracks. Braided steel hoses last longer than rubber.

Can water under laminate or vinyl plank be dried?

Sometimes. Vinyl plank can often be lifted and reused. Laminate that has swollen at the edges usually has to be replaced. See hardwood floor water damage.

Is an appliance leak covered by insurance?

A sudden burst hose is usually covered. A slow leak that went on for weeks may not be.

How long does it take to dry under kitchen cabinets?

Usually 3 to 5 days with cabinet drying equipment.

Can a slow dishwasher leak cause mold?

Yes. A small leak that runs for weeks keeps the subfloor and cabinet base wet, which is ideal for mold. A musty smell under the sink is a common first sign.

Do my kitchen cabinets have to come out?

Usually not. We remove toe kicks and dry inside and under the cabinets. Cabinets come out only if the bases are swollen or moldy.

Is water from a washing machine clean?

No. Washer and dishwasher discharge is Category 2 gray water because it carries soap, food and body soils. It needs antimicrobial cleaning, not just drying, and carpet pad it soaked is usually replaced.

Should I install an automatic water shutoff valve?

It is one of the best upgrades for a laundry room. A sensor on the floor closes the water supply when it detects a leak, so a burst hose becomes a small puddle instead of a flooded floor.

What should I ask before cleanup starts?

Ask for a written scope of work, a moisture map and photos, and confirm the plan with your adjuster. That way you know exactly what will be removed, dried and rebuilt before work begins.
