Pipe Burst? What to Do in the First 30 Minutes

When a pipe bursts, shut off the main water valve first, then cut power to wet areas, drain the lines, move valuables, take photos and call for water removal. Doing these steps in the first 30 minutes can be the difference between drying a room and rebuilding it.

Need help now in Midvale? Call (385) 488-1110 for 24/7 burst pipe cleanup.

Key takeaways

  • Shut off the main water valve first
  • Turn off the water heater and power to wet rooms
  • Drain the lines from the lowest faucet
  • Photograph everything before cleanup
  • Call a plumber and a water damage company at the same time

The 7-Step Burst Pipe Checklist

  • Shut off the main water valve. Turn it clockwise until it stops. It is usually in the basement or utility room near the water heater, or in the meter box near the street.
  • Turn off the water heater. Set gas to "off" or "pilot", or switch off the breaker for an electric heater, so it does not heat an empty tank.
  • Cut power to wet areas at the breaker, only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.
  • Drain the lines. Open the cold faucets on the lowest floor and flush toilets to empty the pipes.
  • Move what you can. Lift rugs, electronics, papers and photos. Put foil or wood blocks under furniture legs.
  • Photograph everything: the burst pipe, the water, and every damaged item and room.
  • Call a plumber and a water damage company at the same time. One fixes the pipe, the other removes the water and dries the house.

What NOT to Do After a Pipe Bursts

  • Do not use a regular household vacuum on water
  • Do not walk through water near outlets, cords or appliances
  • Do not turn the water back on to "test" the pipe
  • Do not use a torch or open flame to thaw a frozen section
  • Do not wait days to start drying; mold can start in 24 to 48 hours
  • Do not throw away damaged items before your adjuster sees them or you photograph them

When to Call a Pro

Call a water damage company if water reached drywall, carpet, a ceiling or more than one room, or if you cannot tell how far it spread. Water inside walls and under floors does not dry with fans alone. See how structural drying works.

Pipe Burst FAQs

How do I turn off the water to my house?

Close the main shut-off valve where the water line enters the house, turning it clockwise. If you cannot find it, the curb valve at the meter box can shut off water to the whole property.

Can I use a pipe after it thaws?

Only after checking it for splits. Turn the water on slowly and watch the pipe. Many frozen pipes split and only leak once they thaw.

Who do I call first, a plumber or a restoration company?

Both, at the same time. The plumber repairs the pipe; the restoration crew removes water and starts drying.

Does insurance cover a burst pipe?

Usually, if the burst was sudden and the home was heated. Read more: insurance claim help.

How fast does water damage get worse?

Within hours, water wicks up drywall and spreads under floors. After 24 to 48 hours, mold and warped wood become likely.

What if I can't find my main shut-off valve?

Use the curb valve in the meter box near the street, or call your water provider's emergency line. Then find the inside valve once things are under control, and label it.

Should I turn off the electricity when a pipe bursts?

Turn off power to the wet areas at the breaker if you can reach the panel from a dry spot. If water is near the panel, stay away and call the power company.

Why did my pipe burst, and could it happen again tonight?

Check the thermostat and furnace first. A furnace that quit, an open garage door or a cold crawl space often caused it. Fix the heat problem right away, or other pipes in the same cold area can freeze and split too.

Should I open windows after a pipe bursts?

Not in winter. Cold air can freeze more pipes and slows drying. In warm, dry weather, a little airflow helps until drying equipment is set up.

Can I keep using water in the rest of the house?

Not until the pipe is repaired or isolated. A plumber can often cap the broken section so the rest of the house has water while drying goes on.
