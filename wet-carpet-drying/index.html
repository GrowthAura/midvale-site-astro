Wet Carpet Drying in Midvale

Wet carpet can often be saved if the water was clean and drying starts within a day or two. Our Midvale crew extracts the water, checks the pad and the floor underneath, replaces what cannot be saved, and dries the carpet and subfloor so it does not smell or grow mold. Call (385) 488-1110.

Quick answer: The type of water decides everything. Clean water from a pipe or supply line usually lets us save the carpet. Gray water from a washer or dishwasher needs cleaning first. Sewage or outside flood water means carpet and pad must be removed.

Why the Carpet Pad Is Usually Replaced

Carpet pad is a sponge. It holds far more water than the carpet above it, dries slowly, and is where mold and odor usually start. New pad is a small part of the job, and replacing it lets the carpet and subfloor dry much faster.

Our Wet Carpet Drying Process in Midvale

  • Find the edge of the water with moisture meters, including under furniture and walls.
  • Extract with truck-mounted and weighted extraction tools.
  • Lift the carpet and remove wet pad if needed.
  • Dry the subfloor or concrete underneath.
  • Float the carpet with air movers under it, plus dehumidifiers.
  • Treat with antimicrobial and deodorize.
  • Install new pad and re-stretch the carpet.

Wet Carpet in Midvale Basements

Many Midvale homes have carpeted basements. Basement concrete stays cold and damp, so carpet over it dries slower and smells sooner. Basement carpet needs the slab underneath dried too, not just the carpet surface.

What Affects How Long Wet Carpet Takes to Dry

  • Water type: clean water dries in place; gray or black water needs cleaning or removal first
  • The pad: replacing a soaked pad speeds up drying a lot
  • What is underneath: concrete in a basement dries slower than a wood subfloor
  • Room size and how many rooms are connected
  • How fast extraction started

Wet Carpet FAQs

How long can carpet stay wet before mold grows?

Mold can start in 24 to 48 hours. Fast extraction and drying matter more than anything else.

Do I need new carpet padding after water damage?

Usually yes. Pad holds a lot of water and is the main source of odor and mold. Replacing it is often the faster, safer choice.

Can I dry wet carpet with fans myself?

Fans dry the top of the carpet but not the pad or the floor underneath. That trapped water is what leads to smells and mold.

Why does my carpet smell after it dried?

The pad or subfloor is likely still damp, or the water was not clean. It needs to be checked with a moisture meter.

How long does wet carpet take to dry?

Usually 2 to 4 days with professional equipment.

Can I walk on carpet while it is drying?

Yes, lightly, with clean socks or shoe covers. Avoid moving furniture back until the carpet and floor underneath are confirmed dry.

Will my carpet shrink or ripple after water damage?

Wet carpet can loosen and ripple. After drying, we re-stretch it so it lies flat again.

What is carpet delamination?

Delamination is when the backing layers of the carpet separate because the glue softened in water. Delaminated carpet will not lie flat or stay stretched, so it usually has to be replaced.

Can I steam clean wet carpet to dry it?

No. A carpet cleaner adds more water and only pulls from the surface. It does not dry the pad or the floor underneath. Extraction plus dehumidifiers is what dries it.

Do you dry carpet in place or take it away?

Clean-water carpet is usually dried in place: we lift it, remove the wet pad and blow air underneath. Carpet soaked with gray or black water is removed for safety.
