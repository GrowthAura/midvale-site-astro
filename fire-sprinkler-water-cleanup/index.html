Sprinkler Went Off? Stop the Flow

  • Make sure there is no fire. Call 911 if there is any doubt.
  • Close the sprinkler control valve only if you are trained to, or call your fire protection company to shut the zone.
  • Cut power to wet areas if it is safe.
  • Move people, inventory and electronics away from the water.
  • Warn tenants below; sprinkler water travels down fast.
  • Call (385) 488-1110.

Why Sprinkler Water Is Dirty

Water in a sprinkler system can sit for years. It picks up rust, scale, bacteria and sometimes antifreeze or corrosion inhibitors. Utah's Division of Water Quality warns that this water should not be sent into storm drains for the same reason. We treat it as contaminated water, clean every surface it touched, and dispose of it properly.

Common Causes of Accidental Sprinkler Discharge

CauseWhat happens
Frozen sprinkler linePipes in unheated spaces freeze and split during Utah cold snaps
Heat near a sprinkler headSpace heaters, cooking or skylights set off a head
Physical damageA ladder, forklift or box knocks a head
Corrosion or defectsOld heads or fittings leak or fail
Testing and maintenanceValves left open or drains not closed

How frozen sprinkler lines fail: ice builds pressure inside the closed pipe, and the break often happens in a section that is not frozen. Many leaks only show up when the ice thaws and water flows again, so a building can look fine during the cold snap and flood the day it warms up.

Fire Sprinkler Water Damage FAQs

How much water does a fire sprinkler head release?

About 20 gallons a minute is common for a single head. In ten minutes that can be hundreds of gallons.

Is fire sprinkler water contaminated?

Yes. It sits in pipes for years and picks up rust and bacteria, and some systems contain antifreeze or chemicals.

Who pays for accidental sprinkler damage?

It depends on the cause and the policies involved. The building's property insurance often covers sudden accidental discharge. We document the loss for every party.

Can a sprinkler line freeze in Utah?

Yes. Lines in unheated attics, garages, loading docks and stairwells can freeze and burst during cold snaps.

How long does it take to dry after a sprinkler discharge?

Usually 3 to 5 days for drying, longer for large buildings or multiple floors.

Should I turn off a fire sprinkler myself?

Only if you are trained and there is no fire. Otherwise call your fire protection company or the fire department to shut down the zone safely. Never leave a building unprotected without telling the fire protection company.

Do ceiling tiles need to be replaced after a sprinkler discharge?

Usually yes. Acoustic ceiling tiles soak up the dirty water, stain and sag, so they are removed and replaced.

Can a dry pipe sprinkler system still leak?

Yes. Condensation can collect and freeze in low spots of a dry pipe system, crack the pipe and make the system lose air pressure. The valve then trips, fills the pipes with water, and it pours out at the crack.

How do I keep sprinkler lines from freezing?

Keep heat on in stairwells, attics, loading docks and other spaces with sprinkler pipes, seal drafts near the lines, and have your fire protection company drain low points in dry systems before winter.

Does the fire department need to know about a discharge?

If the system is shut down, your fire protection company and often the fire department must be told, and a fire watch may be required until it is back in service. Your fire protection company can tell you what Midvale requires.
