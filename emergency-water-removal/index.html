Quick answer: Water removal is the first and most important step of water damage restoration. Every hour water sits, more of it soaks into drywall, wood, carpet pad and insulation. Removing it fast keeps the drying shorter and the repairs smaller.

Before Our Midvale Crew Arrives: 4 Safe Steps

  • Stop the source. Close the main water shut-off valve, or the valve to the leaking fixture.
  • Stay safe around power. Do not walk through water near outlets, cords or appliances. Turn off the breaker for wet rooms if you can reach it from a dry spot.
  • Lift what you can. Put rugs, electronics, papers and photos up high. Place foil or wood blocks under furniture legs.
  • Take photos and video before you clean anything. This helps your insurance claim.

Clean, Gray or Black Water? Why It Changes the Cleanup

The type of water decides what can be saved. Restorers follow the IICRC S500 standard, which sorts water into three categories.

  • Category 1, clean water: a broken supply line or burst pipe. Most materials can be dried and saved if we act fast.
  • Category 2, gray water: washing machine, dishwasher or toilet overflow without waste. Needs cleaning and antimicrobial treatment.
  • Category 3, black water: sewage, outside flood water or water that sat for days. Porous materials like carpet and pad are removed. See sewage cleanup.

Clean water does not stay clean. After 24 to 48 hours it can turn into Category 2 as bacteria grow.

Protecting Your Belongings

When many rooms are wet, we move furniture, rugs and boxes out of the work area or block them up off the floor. Items that need cleaning are photographed and listed for your insurance claim.

Emergency Water Removal FAQs

How fast can you remove the water?

We dispatch a crew the moment you call. Once we arrive, extracting the standing water from a typical home takes about 2 to 6 hours, depending on how much water there is and how many rooms it reached. Drying the structure after that usually takes 3 to 5 days.

Can I use a shop vac myself?

For a small, clean spill, yes. A shop vac cannot pull water out of carpet pad, subfloors or wall cavities, which is where hidden damage starts.

Is standing water dangerous?

It can be. Water near outlets is an electrical risk, and sewage or flood water carries bacteria. Keep children and pets out.

Can my wet carpet be saved?

Carpet wet with clean water can often be dried in place. The pad usually needs to be replaced. See wet carpet drying.

Do you remove water from businesses too?

Yes. We handle offices, retail and multi-unit buildings. See commercial water damage.

Will Utah's dry air dry my house on its own?

No. Dry outdoor air does little for water trapped inside a wall cavity, under a subfloor or in attic insulation. Winter inversions also hold cold, damp air in the Salt Lake Valley. Controlled drying with dehumidifiers is what gets materials back to a dry reading.

What is the difference between water removal and water mitigation?

Water removal (extraction) pulls out the standing water. Water mitigation is the full first response: extraction, removing what cannot be saved, setting drying equipment and stopping mold. Restoration is the repair work that follows.

Does insurance pay for emergency water removal?

Most homeowners policies pay for removal and drying after a sudden leak, such as a burst pipe or appliance failure. Flooding from outside usually needs separate flood coverage. We send your adjuster photos, moisture maps and daily drying logs.
