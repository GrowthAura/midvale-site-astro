Ice Dam Water Damage Cleanup in Midvale

An ice dam is a ridge of ice at the edge of your roof that traps melting snow. The trapped water backs up under the shingles and leaks into the attic, outside walls and ceilings. Our Midvale crew finds where it went, dries it and stops mold before it starts. Call (385) 488-1110.

Quick answer: If a ceiling or the top of an outside wall starts leaking during or after a snowy week, and you see thick ice or long icicles at the roof edge, you likely have an ice dam. The leak you see is usually smaller than the water hidden in the insulation and wall cavity.

How Ice Dams Form on Midvale Roofs

  • Heat escapes from the house into the attic.
  • The warm roof melts the snow above it.
  • Meltwater runs down to the cold roof edge (the eaves) and freezes.
  • The ice ridge grows and blocks the next meltwater.
  • Water pools behind the ice and seeps under the shingles into your home.

Northern Utah's freeze-thaw cycles make this common. Sunny days melt snow on the roof, and cold nights refreeze it at the edge.

Signs of Ice Dam Water Damage Inside Your Home

  • Thick ice or heavy icicles along the gutters
  • Water stains on the ceiling near outside walls
  • Peeling paint or bubbling drywall at the top of a wall
  • Damp or flattened attic insulation
  • Water around window frames on upper floors
  • A musty smell in upstairs rooms or closets

Our Ice Dam Water Damage Process in Midvale

  • Inspect the attic, walls and ceilings and map every wet area.
  • Remove soaked insulation and drywall that cannot be dried.
  • Dry wall cavities and the ceiling with dehumidifiers, air movers and wall cavity drying.
  • Apply antimicrobial to framing to stop mold.
  • Replace insulation, repair drywall and paint.
  • Give you photos and moisture readings for your insurance claim.

How to Remove an Ice Dam Safely (and What Not to Do)

Do not chop ice with a hammer or shovel; it tears shingles. Avoid rock salt, which damages the roof and plants. A roofing or snow removal pro can melt channels with low-pressure steam. Once the water can drain, we take care of the damage inside.

A safer DIY channel: fill a nylon stocking with calcium chloride ice melt (not rock salt) and lay it across the ice dam so it hangs over the gutter. It melts a path for the trapped water to drain. Work from the ground with a roof rake, never from a ladder on ice.

How to Prevent Ice Dams in a Utah Winter

  • Add attic insulation and seal air leaks around lights, fans and the attic hatch
  • Improve attic ventilation so the roof stays cold
  • Rake snow off the first few feet of the roof after big storms
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear before winter
  • Ask a roofer about heat cables on problem edges

Ice Dam Water Damage FAQs

How do I know I have an ice dam?

Look for a thick ridge of ice and large icicles at the roof edge, plus leaks or stains near outside walls during snowy weather.

Can ice dams cause mold?

Yes. Water trapped in insulation and wall cavities can grow mold within 24 to 48 hours, often out of sight.

Is ice dam damage covered by insurance?

Many homeowners policies cover sudden water damage inside the home from an ice dam. Removing the ice itself or fixing worn roofing may not be covered. We document the damage for your adjuster.

Should I wait for spring to fix the leak?

No. Wet insulation and drywall can grow mold and rot all winter. Dry it now and fix the roof cause when weather allows.

Why does the leak stop when it gets cold?

The meltwater refreezes overnight. The leak returns on the next sunny day, so the damage keeps building.

Can ice dam water reach the basement?

Yes. Water that gets past the shingles can run down inside an outside wall from the attic to the floor framing, and show up as a wet baseboard or carpet on a lower level. We check every floor under a roof leak.

How much attic insulation stops ice dams in Utah?

The Salt Lake Valley is in climate zone 5, where ENERGY STAR suggests about R-49 to R-60 in the attic. Sealing air leaks around lights, bath fans and the attic hatch matters as much as adding insulation.

Can ice dam damage collapse a ceiling?

It can. Soaked drywall loses its strength, and wet insulation adds weight. If the ceiling sags or bulges, keep everyone out of the room and call us right away.
